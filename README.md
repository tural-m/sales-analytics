💰 Sales Analytics Dashboard

End-to-end sales analytics project — live Salesforce data connected directly to Power BI, normalized in Power Query, and visualized across 4 executive dashboard pages.


🎯 Business Problem
A retail business operating across Canada with customers in multiple segments and channels needed clarity on:

Why revenue and orders were declining month over month from January to April
Which segments, channels, provinces, and product categories were driving performance
Where the conversion funnel was leaking — from sessions to purchases
Which promotions were generating the most revenue
How to prioritize actions to stabilize and grow performance


🔍 Key Findings
💰 Revenue & Profitability

Total revenue of $29,758 across 308 orders at a 34% profit margin (Sales page)
Revenue declined steadily from $10.1K in January to $3.7K in April — a 63% drop
Profit followed the same trend — from $3.5K in January to $1.1K in April
Web traffic data shows 236K sessions generating only 7.8K orders — a 3.3% end-to-end conversion rate

🧩 Segment & Channel

SMB is the strongest segment at $11.4K, outperforming Consumer ($9.5K) and Enterprise ($8.9K)
Retail dominates at 78.55% of revenue vs Online at 21.45% — significant online growth opportunity
Alberta leads provinces at $10.6K combined across Calgary and Edmonton

🛒 Conversion Funnel

236K sessions → 19.25K add-to-carts → 10.15K checkouts → 7.8K orders
Cart abandonment rate: 93% — the single biggest revenue leak in the business
Conversion rate averages only 0.06 across all months

🏷️ Promotions

Surprisingly, no promotion drives the highest revenue at $7.6K
Discount ($6.8K) and Loyalty ($6.4K) are close behind
BOGO performs weakest at $4.4K — potentially cannibalizing margin without driving volume

📦 Products

Lighting subcategory dominates in both Kitchen ($5.4K) and Accessories ($5.6K)
Appliances show strong performance in Kitchen ($3.4K) but weaker in Accessories ($2.3K)
Home category is the most evenly distributed across subcategories
